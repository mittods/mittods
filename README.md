### Martín Maza Delgado

Final-year Civil Engineering in Computer Science student at Universidad Austral de Chile (UACh),
continuing into the Master in Informatics (2027–28). I build and operate distributed systems with
AI components, and I am interested in **autonomic computing**: systems that monitor, diagnose and
adapt themselves, especially when some of their components are learned models.

#### Current work (mostly in private repositories)

- **Industrial IoT monitoring platform** (Honner Chile, 2024–present): real-time monitoring of
  manufacturing machinery (sensors, PLCs, edge computers) with an edge–cloud architecture.
  Exploring agent-based integrity monitoring of the platform. *Company code, not public.*
- **LLM pipelines for research** (Fondecyt 11260438, UACh): multi-agent pipelines for systematic
  literature reviews, with an independent step that verifies each extracted claim against its
  source. *Private until the related papers are published.*
- **Di-Judge**: UACh's online judge for competitive programming (FastAPI, Celery/Redis, Docker
  sandboxes; C++ via pybind11 and OpenMP). *Institutional repository; experiments are public below.*
